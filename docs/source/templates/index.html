Plotter ready

LineDream was originally designed for pen plotters, so its SVGs work well on them out of the box. It's just as useful for general SVG creation.

Shapes are objects

Line, Circle, Rectangle, Arc and friends each carry their own stroke, fill and vertices. Subclass BaseShape to make your own.

Transform anything

Call rotate(), scale() or transform() on any shape, around any origin. No push/pop matrix bookkeeping.

Plotter-ready SVG

Set Canvas.units = 'mm' for real-world sizes, and use Group to export Inkscape layers, one per pen.

Single-stroke text

Text renders with Hershey fonts: lettering made of strokes, not outlines, so a pen draws each character once.
